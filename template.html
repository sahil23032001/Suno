Hindi PDF to audiobook

अपनी हिंदी PDF को सुनिए, पढ़िए नहीं।

Open a Hindi PDF and it becomes an audiobook. Your phone's own Hindi voice reads each page aloud while the sentence being spoken stays highlighted.

The file is read on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • 1Text PDFs only. Works with documents that were generated by a computer. A scan or a photo of paper has no text to read.
  • 2Needs a Hindi voice. Android: Settings → General management → Text-to-speech → install Hindi under Google Speech Services. Tap the gear above to check what you have.
  • 3Add to home screen. Chrome menu → Add to Home screen, and it opens like a normal app.

No Hindi voice?

Android reads Hindi through Google Speech Services. If the list above has no Hindi voice:
  1. Open Settings and search for Text-to-speech.
  2. Set the engine to Google Speech Services, open its settings, and install the Hindi voice data.
  3. Come back here and reopen this panel.
